Rule 2 – Exploits & Modifications

The use of exploits, scripts, or any unauthorized tools that modify core gameplay mechanics is strictly prohibited.

Note: Keyboard macros or simple input hardware automation (such as auto-clicking toolsets) are fully allowed.
Permanent Ban

Rule 7 – Teaming with Exploiters

Teaming with or assisting exploiters is treated as exploiting. This includes being present inside servers affiliated with AoH Exploits.
  • First Offense: 30-day ban.
  • Repeated Offense: Same permanent punishment as the primary exploiter.
30-Day / Perm Ban

Rule 11 – Bug / Glitch Abuse

Abusing unintended game bugs, map glitches, or broken scripts to gain a functional competitive advantage over other players is not allowed.
Temporary to Permanent

Rule 12 & 13 – Troll & Tank Stats

  • Troll Stats: Allowed. However, using them to maliciously harm players (e.g., dragging or throwing players into the void) is completely forbidden.
  • Tank Stats: Fully allowed as long as you are outside active player combat areas on public game servers.
Allowed Builds

Conduct & Behavior

Rule 3 – Discrimination & Behavior

Discrimination or offensive behavior is strictly prohibited in-game. This zero-tolerance policy covers:
  • Racism, Sexism, & Homophobia
  • Ableism & Body-shaming
  • The use of offensive slurs
Permanent Ban

Rule 4 – Death Threats

Sending severe threats or encouraging community members to self-harm (including chat shorthand text variants such as "kys") is strictly forbidden.
  • First Offense: 90-day ban.
  • Repeated Offense: Permanent account ban.
90-Day / Perm Ban

Rule 5 – Doxxing

Sharing, publishing, or leaking private personal information regarding any community member or staff layer is prohibited.

Exception: Reposting information that is already fully public may result in a scaled 30-day ban.
Permanent Ban

Rule 14 & 15 – Chat Management

  • Admin Harassment: Harassing administrators, moderation teams, or game developers will result in a ban.
  • Voice Chat: All conduct rules apply while using VC. Disruptive behavior, excessive noise loops, or audio abuse is forbidden.
Mute / Ban Hold

Account & Staff

Level Transfer Protocol

You can request level transfers between your accounts, but levels replace each other instead of stacking.

Example: Main Level 7000 + Alt Level 2500 → transferring drops Main to 0 and sets Alt to 7000.
  • Level stacking allowed? NO.
  • Transfer to others? NO — only to your own alts.
System Transfer

Roblox Ban Recovery

Lost data due to a global Roblox platform ban?

YES, you can recover. Staff will check the backend records to verify your past level data along with your exact last recorded cash metrics.
Data Recovery

Banned Main Account Policy

Can you pull levels off a banned account?

NO. All official level transfers must originate from a clean account that is completely unbanned.
Transfer Blocked

Rule 6 – False Reporting

Submitting fake reports or providing fabricated evidence to staff layers is not allowed. Violating this triggers the mirror rule.

Example: Fabricating proof of another player exploiting will get you banned permanently for exploiting.
Mirror Punishment

Rule 8 – Account Responsibility

Account sharing is allowed, but all active profile behavior remains the responsibility of the account owner. Claims such as "someone else used my account" are rejected.

Appeals are completely blocked until the 90-day appeal window passes.
Owner Liable

Rule 10 – Alternate Accounts (Alts)

Alternate profiles are allowed. However, using alternate accounts to bypass active bans or punishments is completely illegal.
Profile Blacklist

Rule 9 – Joking About Exploits

Publicly joking, baiting, or bragging about exploiting can be treated as a real confession and a valid system violation.
Temporary Ban

Official Appeals System

Appeals are processed through the official Age of Heroes Discord server. Reviews are not guaranteed to clear.

Crucial Limit: Appeals are only considered after you have been banned for a minimum of 90 days.
Discord Only
